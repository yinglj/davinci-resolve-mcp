# P1-03: Auto Color Grading & Audio Processing

## 概要
提供自动调色与音频处理流水线：基于风格样例、LUT 或模型应用分级；集成 TTS、音频降噪与音量均衡。

## 目标
- AutoColor: style-transfer-like grading, LUT application and refinement
- Audio: TTS for voiceover, noise reduction, loudness normalization
- Integrate with Planner and Executor to schedule color/audio passes

6-10 天
